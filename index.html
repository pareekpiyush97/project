04 — How it runs

Four
Stages

Every car takes the same route through the studio. No shortcuts, no surprises on the invoice.

STAGE 01

Intake & Inspection

Paint depth readings, panel-by-panel photos and a written scope before a single pad touches the car.

STAGE 02

Decontamination

Chemical and mechanical decon, then a full strip wash so we correct paint — not the grime sitting on it.

STAGE 03

Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing under calibrated lighting until the finish holds a clean reflection.

STAGE 04

Protection

Film or coating applied in a filtered booth, cured, then re-inspected before the keys come back to you.
